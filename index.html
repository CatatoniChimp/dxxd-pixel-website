FAQ

Questions? Start here.

How do I get a repair quote?

Send us your device model, the problem you're experiencing and any useful photos. We'll then discuss the repair and provide a quote where possible.

Do I need to visit a shop?

DXXD PIXEL operates using a convenient collection and return model rather than relying on a traditional retail storefront.

What devices do you repair?

We handle a range of phone, tablet and selected laptop repairs, along with diagnostic services. Contact us with your device and fault and we'll confirm whether we can help.

Will I know the price before the repair?

We'll explain the expected cost before carrying out agreed repair work. Some faults may require diagnosis before an accurate repair price can be confirmed.
